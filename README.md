# Specialized Urban Locations

> **Placeholder** — this README is a work in progress.

A comprehensive EU5 mod integrating multiple economic, military, and political subsystems under a unified framework.

## Systems

### Economy & Production
- **Specialization System** — Location specializations (mining, farming, woodland, gathering, commercial) driven by resources, terrain, and market conditions
- **Provisions** — Custom food/provisions commodity replacing vanilla food, with RGO building management and GDP/wages/demand overhaul
- **Estate Building Maintenance** — Estates pay building upkeep proportional to estate power, using market prices
- **Trade Maintenance Efficiency** — Reframes merchant maintenance as multiplicatively compounding efficiency

### Military
- **War Momentum** — Monthly war penalties: fort defense loss from strength depletion, war exhaustion from occupation/pressure
- **Combat Overcrowding** — Per-tick battle penalty for armies exceeding frontage capacity
- **Levy Artillery** — Non-buildable levy-only artillery units for ages 2-6
- **Levy Combat Ability Removed** — Replaces vanilla levy combat efficiency with discipline/morale equivalents

### Governance & Diplomacy
- **Minting / Gold Standard** — Price-based minting profitability, debasement/rebasement mechanics, currency laws
- **Population-Based Integration** — Conquered locations integrate faster when underpopulated
- **Land-Focus Reforms** — Government reforms gated on land_focus societal value

### Infrastructure
- **Proximity & Control Overhaul** — Rebalanced distance/road/river effects on proximity propagation
