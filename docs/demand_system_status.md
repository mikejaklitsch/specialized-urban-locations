# Demand System Redesign — Status & Next Steps

## What Was Built (April 27, 2026)

### Continuous Percentage Demand Model (Complete, Working)
Budget-filling demand tier system. Each tier gets a weight-based share of WPP via `WPP × weight / (A + B×WPP)`. Constant-weight tiers (necessity/basic/common) saturate at high wealth. Growth-weight tiers (upper/luxury/exotic) scale with WPP².

**Config** (`tools/demand_calculator.py`):
```python
POOR_SHARES  = {"necessity": 0.55, "basic": 0.30, "common": 0.15}
RICH_SHARES  = {"upper": 0.45, "luxury": 0.35, "exotic": 0.20}
CROSSOVER_WPP = 1.0
```

**Key files:**
- `in_game/common/script_values/sul_pop_demand_wpp.txt` — demand tier formulas
- `in_game/common/goods_demand/sul_pop_demands.txt` — per-good demand entries
- `in_game/common/goods/sul_goods.txt` — demand_add values (no tier_weight)
- `tools/demand_calculator.py` — generates goods, demand maps, GUI tooltip

### WPP Model (Complete, Working, Tuning Needed)

**Architecture:**
```
WPP = wage_component + capital_returns_component

wage_component = total_wage_pool × estate_power / num_pops_of_type
  - Wage pool = sum of all 6 pop type wage bills at the location
  - Distributed by political power (local_relative_estate_power)
  - Upper estates capture commoner wages; commoners split by wage bill share

capital_returns = estate_gold × 2% / country_pops × asset_share × power
  - Flat 2% annual return (@sul_return_rate = 0.02)
  - Returns paid to estate as gold (add_gold_to_estate)
  - Commoners get flat_wealth = estate_gold × 2% (not per-pop)
```

**Key files:**
- `in_game/common/scripted_effects/sul_gdp_update.txt` — WPP computation, budget pressure, capital returns
- `in_game/common/scripted_effects/sul_estate_wages.txt` — wage payments, inter-estate transfers
- `in_game/common/script_values/sul_budget_pressure.txt` — per-estate spending multiplier
- `in_game/common/auto_modifiers/sul_base_values.txt` — base wages (nobles=5, clergy=2, burghers=1, soldiers=0.5, laborers=0.25, peasants=0.05)
- `in_game/common/script_values/sul_tooltip_values.txt` — live-computed tooltip values

### GDP System (Complete, Working)

Per-location gross output computation. Building hooks track which goods are produced locally via `sul_local_goods` variable map. Yearly computation iterates the map using `every_key_in_variable_map`, sums `goods_output × price_in_market`. Asset share = local/national GDP.

**Key files:**
- `in_game/common/scripted_effects/sul_gdp_effects.txt` — yearly GDP update, init
- `in_game/common/scripted_effects/sul_gdp_generated_effects.txt` — auto-generated per-good tracking effects, dispatch, init building scan
- `tools/generate_building_hooks.py` — extended to generate GDP hooks alongside EPBM hooks

### Tooltip (Complete, Working)

Generated by `demand_calculator.py --gui`. Columns: Pop Type | Wages | Returns | WPP. Each column has hover breakdown. Demand breakdown on WPP hover shows per-tier goods.

**Key files:**
- `in_game/gui/shared/aaa_sul_location_tooltips.gui` — generated from marker `### WPDO: POP DEMAND BUDGET`
- `in_game/common/script_values/sul_pop_demand_table.txt` — 36 per-tier-per-pop values for demand breakdown
- `in_game/common/scripted_effects/sul_init_effects.txt` — `sul_init_demand_maps` populates `sul_da_*` global maps

### Inter-Estate Wage Transfers (Complete, Working)

Upper estates pay a portion of commoner wages to the peasant estate, scaled by enfranchisement × commoner political power. Accumulated per-location during the WPP update, executed after wage payments.

**Key file:** `in_game/common/scripted_effects/sul_estate_wages.txt`

### EU5 Engine Lessons Learned

- `add_to_variable_map` does NOT accept computed `value = { }` blocks — must pre-compute into local variables
- `clamp_variable` syntax uncertain — use explicit `if/set_variable` for reliable floors
- `every_key_in_variable_map` uses `variable =` not `name =`
- `error_log` interpolation: only `SCOPE.GetLocalVariable('name').GetValue` works — no `GetVariable` for regular variables. Copy to locals first.
- `pop_type_population_in_country` is a comparison trigger, not a value. Use `"pop_type_population_in_country(pop_type:X)"` quoted parametric syntax for values.
- GUI files: BOM stacking from repeated `utf-8-sig` writes. Read with `utf-8-sig` (strips BOM), write with `utf-8-sig` (adds one).
- EPBM PM rename: must check existing prefix to prevent stacking across generator runs.

## Proposed: Location-Based Estate Wealth

### Concept

Replace the country-level estate gold pool as the capital returns source with per-location **estate assets** — physical wealth (land, workshops, trade networks, church property) placed at specific locations. Crown treasury would also be localized, scaled by local crown power (mechanism TBD).

### Motivation

Estate gold is invisible, indestructible, and geographically uniform. Location-based assets create:
- **Economic warfare**: sieges and devastation destroy physical wealth
- **Geographic variation**: wealthy cities have more assets than rural backwaters
- **Recovery cost**: assets rebuild slowly from GDP, making war economically painful
- **Strategic depth**: target enemy economic centers, protect your own

### Architecture

**Two types of capital:**
- **Liquid capital** (estate gold): engine-managed treasury. Funds armies, buildings, estate actions. Country-level, unaffected by warfare.
- **Fixed capital** (location assets): per-location, per-estate. Generates returns for WPP. Destroyed by warfare/devastation. Rebuilt from GDP growth.

**Variables per location:**
- `sul_noble_assets`, `sul_clergy_assets`, `sul_burgher_assets`
- Possibly `sul_crown_assets` (scaled by local crown power — mechanism TBD)

**Asset accumulation (yearly, during GDP pass):**
```
asset_growth = location_gdp_output × power_share × growth_rate
location_assets += asset_growth
```
Assets accumulate where the economic activity is, weighted by who controls it.

**Asset-driven returns (replaces current country-level returns):**
```
noble_capital_return = sul_noble_assets × return_rate / num_nobles
```
No more asset_share computation needed — assets ARE the local presence.

**Asset destruction:**
- Devastation: `assets *= (1 - devastation_fraction)` on monthly tick
- Siege: large one-time destruction via `on_siege_won` (already in `sul_hardcoded.txt`)
- Occupation: ongoing drain via war momentum monthly pulse
- Looting: one-time event-based destruction

**Recovery:**
Assets grow from GDP accumulation. Devastated locations have low GDP → low growth → slow recovery. Fast to destroy, slow to rebuild.

### What Changes

| Current | Proposed |
|---|---|
| `estate_gold × 2% / country_pops` | `location_assets × return_rate / num_pops` |
| Country-level returns distributed by asset_share × power | Local returns from local assets |
| Asset share from GDP ratio | Not needed — assets are already local |
| Estate gold indestructible | Location assets destroyed by war |
| No geographic wealth variation | Explicit per-location wealth |

### Performance

- +3 variables per location (one per upper estate), +1 if crown
- Yearly: +3 writes per location in GDP pass
- Monthly: +3 multiplies per devastated location
- On-action: +3 sets per siege event
- Removes: asset_share computation, country_pop_* accumulation
- Net: roughly equivalent ops, better economic model

### Enrichment Tier (Savings)

Add a 7th demand tier — **Enrichment** — as a growth-weight tier in the continuous percentage model. It competes for budget share using the same curve but instead of generating goods demand, the "demand" flows to estates as gold income (via the existing wage payment system). This IS the savings mechanism.

**Target:** ~50% of budget at WPP=15, nearly zero at low WPP. The rich save massively; the poor can't afford to.

**Flow:** Enrichment value × num_pops → paid to the estate that owns those pops, just like wages. When the location asset system is implemented, this estate gold naturally becomes the source of location wealth — the treasury that gets distributed to locations and can be destroyed by war.

**Why this works:**
- Uses the existing demand model infrastructure (same formula, same budget-fill guarantee)
- No new system needed — enrichment is just estate gold income from pop savings
- The savings rate is emergent from WPP (rich save more, poor save nothing)
- Closes the growth loop: income → enrichment → estate gold → capital returns → higher income
- War destroys location assets (once implemented), breaking the compound growth
- Enfranchisement shifts who saves by shifting who earns

**Weight calibration needed:** The growth weights (upper/luxury/exotic/enrichment) must be rebalanced so that at WPP=15, enrichment captures ~50% while the existing consumption tiers share the remainder. The `demand_calculator.py` RICH_SHARES config handles this — just add an enrichment entry and adjust the others down.

**Implementation:** Minimal — add the tier to `sul_pop_demand_wpp.txt`, add `@sul_w_enrichment` macro, compute `sul_demand_enrichment` as a growth tier. In `sul_update_location_wpp`, the enrichment value × num_pops is accumulated into wage bills and paid to estates. No demand_add entries needed (enrichment doesn't demand goods). The demand_calculator needs the 7th tier in RICH_SHARES and updated GUI generation.

### Open Questions

1. **Crown assets**: how to determine local crown power? Engine may expose this through estate power for crown estate, or it may need to be derived from control/autonomy.
2. **Initial seeding**: how much starting assets per location? Could derive from location_net_building_profit × multiplier, or from the first GDP pass.
3. **Asset cap**: should assets have a maximum per location? Perhaps proportional to building levels or development.
4. **Interaction with EPBM**: estate building maintenance already drains estate gold. Should it also drain location assets? Or keep EPBM on liquid capital only.
5. **Commoner assets**: do commoners get location-based assets, or keep the flat_wealth supplement from estate gold?
