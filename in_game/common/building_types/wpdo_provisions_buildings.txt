# ============================================
# PROVISIONS BUILDINGS
# ============================================
# Provisions system flow:
#   1. Farmer's Market converts raw food goods → provisions (trade good)
#   2. Windmill supplements with grain-milling provisions
#   3. Provisions trades on the market like any good
#   4. Granary consumes provisions → local_monthly_food (1.0 provisions = 30 food)
#   5. Rural granary does the same at smaller scale (0.08 provisions = 2.5 food)
#
# ============================================
# FARMER'S MARKET RECIPES
# ============================================
# All recipes normalized to 1.80 gold input cost → 33% profit margin at 2.4 provisions price.
# Differentiation comes from RGO output scaling, not farmer's market margins.
